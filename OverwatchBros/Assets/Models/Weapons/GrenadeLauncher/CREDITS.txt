Used in the game as Honza's grenade launcher (Assets/Editor/GrenadeLauncherSetup.cs -> Prefab/Weapons/Held_GrenadeLauncher.prefab).
Edited in Blender: the yellow muzzle cap is cut out as a separate part "MuzzleCap" with its origin at the hinge
(it flips open on every shot, see Scripts/MuzzleCap.cs); the ammo crate is a separate part "Magazine" and the four
grenades in it are "Grenade_1".."Grenade_4" sharing one mesh (they move on every shot, see Scripts/GrenadeFeed.cs);
the gear-shift lever on top is "CockingLever" with its origin at the bottom of the stick (pulled back on reload).
Otherwise unchanged (13.7k triangles, 1k textures).
